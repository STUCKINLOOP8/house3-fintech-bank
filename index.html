About the Risk Engine

The IronVault Risk Engine processes transaction data against a multi-layer anomaly detection model trained on historical fraud patterns. Scores are updated in real-time as new transactions are ingested. A fraud score below 0.20 is considered low risk. Anomaly counts above 5 trigger automatic escalation to the compliance review queue.
